Sycamore Ridge Elementary Area Homes: Common Questions

How much does a house in the Sycamore Ridge Elementary area cost?

In the last 12 months, 9 single-family homes in the Sycamore Ridge Elementary attendance area sold at a median of $2,490,000 (about $830 per sq ft), versus $2,625,000 for all Carmel Valley houses. Over the last 5 years, 50 sold for between $1,800,000 and $10,895,000.

Which neighborhoods are in the Sycamore Ridge Elementary area?

Based on where homes have sold, the area includes Soleil at Bordeaux, Wild Horse, Bordeaux, Bougainvillea, Hunt Club, The Preserve, Arabella, Triple Crown, and more. Soleil at Bordeaux has had the most sales. Boundaries can split a neighborhood, so check the exact address.

What middle and high school go with Sycamore Ridge Elementary?

High school is Torrey Pines High (San Dieguito Union HSD); Canyon Crest Academy and San Dieguito Academy are available by lottery. Middle school depends on the address, so confirm it with San Dieguito Union HSD.
