## Carmel Valley Neighborhoods: Common Questions

### How many single-family neighborhoods are in Carmel Valley?

This map shows 127 single-family neighborhoods in Carmel Valley (San Diego, 92130), with more than 11,300 homes between them. They are spread across 8 areas, and 36 of them are in Pacific Highlands Ranch.

### Which Carmel Valley neighborhoods have the lowest and highest home prices?

Among neighborhoods with at least three house sales in the last 12 months, San Remo had the lowest median ($1.91M) and Rancho Pacifica the highest ($5.93M). Lot size, home age and school area explain most of the gap.

### What are the largest single-family neighborhoods in Carmel Valley?

By number of homes, the largest are Heights (566 homes), Heights (566 homes), Palacio Del Mar (422 homes). Bigger neighborhoods have more sales each year, which makes prices easier to compare.

### Where are the newest houses in Carmel Valley?

Pacific Highlands Ranch, east of the SR-56, was built out from the 2000s onward and still has some of the newest homes. Many of its neighborhoods pay Mello-Roos on top of property tax, so compare the full yearly cost.
