Pacific Highlands Ranch Homes: Common Questions

How much does a house in Pacific Highlands Ranch cost?

In the last 12 months, 58 single-family homes in Pacific Highlands Ranch sold at a median of $2,470,000 (about $877 per sq ft), below the Carmel Valley median of $2,625,000. Over the last 5 years, 278 sold for between $1,270,000 and $6,190,000.

Which neighborhoods are in Pacific Highlands Ranch?

Pacific Highlands Ranch has 36 single-family neighborhoods, including Sendero Collection, Portico, Arabella, Soleil at Bordeaux, Almeria, Elms. Among those with at least three sales in the last 12 months, Arabella had the lowest median ($1.95M) and Vista Del Mar the highest ($3.92M).

How old are the homes in Pacific Highlands Ranch?

Homes that sold in the last 5 years were built between 1996 and 2023, with a median year of 2016.

Do homes in Pacific Highlands Ranch pay Mello-Roos?

Many Pacific Highlands Ranch neighborhoods pay Mello-Roos (a special tax for local infrastructure) on top of regular property tax. The amount differs by neighborhood and year, so check the tax bill on every listing.

What schools serve Pacific Highlands Ranch?

Elementary school depends on the street: Pacific Sky Elementary (32% of recent sales), Sycamore Ridge Elementary (8% of recent sales), and Solana Beach School District schools for much of the rest. High school is Torrey Pines High, with Canyon Crest Academy and San Dieguito Academy by lottery. Confirm any address with the district.

How fast do homes sell in Pacific Highlands Ranch?

Over the last 12 months, the median home sold in 13 days on market.
