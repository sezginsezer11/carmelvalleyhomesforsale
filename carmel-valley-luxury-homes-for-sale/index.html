Carmel Valley homes over $3 million: the market now

In the last 12 months, 70 of 206 single-family homes in Carmel Valley sold for $3 million or more. That is about 34% of all house sales, up from 32% in the 12 months before. 20 sold for $4 million or more and 6 for $5 million or more.

The typical luxury home here has 5 bedrooms (35 of the 70 sales), about 4,176 sq ft of living space and a lot of about 10,507 sq ft. They were built between 1978 and 2023, with a median year of 2003.

High end homes in Carmel Valley still move quickly: half of the $3M+ sales went under contract within 11 days on market. That leaves little time to decide, so compare a home with recent sales in its own neighborhood before you tour it.

What counts as a luxury home in Carmel Valley?
