Buying a House in Carmel Valley: Common Questions

What is the median price of a single-family home in Carmel Valley?

$2,625,000, based on 209 single-family homes that closed in the last 12 months in ZIP 92130 (about $879 per square foot). Condos and townhomes are not included.

Can you still buy a house in Carmel Valley for under $2 million?

Yes, but there are few of them. 26 of 209 single-family sales in the last 12 months closed under $2M, mostly smaller or older homes and newer homes on compact lots. They tend to sell quickly, so set up alerts and be ready to act.

How fast do Carmel Valley houses sell?

The median single-family home sold in 10 days on market over the last 12 months. Well-priced homes in popular school areas often go under contract in the first week.

Which part of Carmel Valley has the most home sales?

South of Del Mar Heights Road had the most single-family sales in the last 12 months (48). Among areas with at least five sales, Del Mar Mesa had the highest median at $4,275,000.

Is this site for condos and townhomes too?

No. This site covers single-family homes only. For condos and townhomes, including HOA fees and Mello-Roos, see Carmel Valley condos and townhomes. For every property type in one search, see all Carmel Valley listings.
