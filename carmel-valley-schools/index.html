Two elementary school districts

Western and central Carmel Valley is in the Del Mar Union School District. Much of Pacific Highlands Ranch, Torrey Highlands and some neighborhoods north of Del Mar Heights Road are in Solana Beach School District: Solana Ranch, Solana Highlands, Solana Santa Fe, and Carmel Creek (K–3) with Solana Pacific for grades 4–6. Both send students on to San Dieguito Union High School District. Look up an exact address with Del Mar Union School District or the Solana Beach SD school locator.

Carmel Valley Schools: Common Questions

Which elementary school serves my Carmel Valley address?

Most of western and central Carmel Valley is in the Del Mar Union School District: Sage Canyon, Pacific Sky, Ocean Air, Ashley Falls, Sycamore Ridge, Torrey Hills and Carmel Del Mar elementary schools. Much of Pacific Highlands Ranch and Torrey Highlands is in Solana Beach School District (Solana Ranch, Solana Highlands, Solana Santa Fe, and Carmel Creek with Solana Pacific). Boundaries can split a neighborhood, so check the exact address with the district locator.

What middle and high school do Carmel Valley homes go to?

Carmel Valley is in San Dieguito Union High School District. Middle school is Carmel Valley Middle or Pacific Trails Middle depending on the address. The assigned high school is Torrey Pines High, and Canyon Crest Academy and San Dieguito Academy are open by lottery.

Do homes cost more in some school areas?

Yes. Prices follow home size, age and lot as much as the school, but each school page shows the 12-month median and what houses inside that boundary sold for, so you can compare like for like.

How do I search for homes in one school area?

Tap the school on the map, then See homes. Each school page lists the neighborhoods inside the boundary and recent sales. Call or email Sez Sezer for listings inside a specific boundary, including homes not yet on the market.
