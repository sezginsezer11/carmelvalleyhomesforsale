Torrey Hills Homes: Common Questions

How much does a house in Torrey Hills cost?

In the last 12 months, 25 single-family homes in Torrey Hills sold at a median of $2,560,000 (about $868 per sq ft), below the Carmel Valley median of $2,625,000. They sold for between $1,600,667 and $4,800,000.

Which neighborhoods are in Torrey Hills?

Torrey Hills has 17 single-family neighborhoods, including Sands, Torrey View, Torrey Woods Estates, Sansonnet, Monte Claire, Gables. Among those with at least three sales in the last 12 months, Sands had the lowest median ($1.92M) and Torrey View the highest ($2.44M).

How old are the homes in Torrey Hills?

Homes that sold in the last 5 years were built between 1978 and 2015, with a median year of 2000.

What schools serve Torrey Hills?

Elementary school depends on the street: Ocean Air Elementary (63% of recent sales), Torrey Hills Elementary (37% of recent sales). High school is Torrey Pines High, with Canyon Crest Academy and San Dieguito Academy by lottery. Confirm any address with the district.

How fast do homes sell in Torrey Hills?

Over the last 12 months, the median home sold in 10 days on market.
