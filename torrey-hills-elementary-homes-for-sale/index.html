Torrey Hills Elementary Area Homes: Common Questions

How much does a house in the Torrey Hills Elementary area cost?

In the last 12 months, 7 single-family homes in the Torrey Hills Elementary attendance area sold at a median of $2,450,000 (about $868 per sq ft), versus $2,625,000 for all Carmel Valley houses. They sold for between $1,949,000 and $3,100,000.

Which neighborhoods are in the Torrey Hills Elementary area?

Based on where homes have sold, the area includes Torrey View, Shores, Searidge, St. Augustine, Torrey Point, La Strada, Vantage Point, Sea Country. Torrey View has had the most sales. Boundaries can split a neighborhood, so check the exact address.

What middle and high school go with Torrey Hills Elementary?

High school is Torrey Pines High (San Dieguito Union HSD); Canyon Crest Academy and San Dieguito Academy are available by lottery. Middle school depends on the address, so confirm it with San Dieguito Union HSD.

How do I confirm a home is in the Torrey Hills Elementary boundary?

Use the Del Mar Union School District school locator with the exact address before you make an offer. Boundaries change, and this map is for research only.

How fast do homes sell in the Torrey Hills Elementary area?

Over the last 12 months, the median home sold in 26 days on market. Homes in sought-after school areas often go under contract in the first week.
