Ashley Falls Elementary Area Homes: Common Questions

How much does a house in the Ashley Falls Elementary area cost?

In the last 12 months, 17 single-family homes in the Ashley Falls Elementary attendance area sold at a median of $2,800,000 (about $821 per sq ft), versus $2,625,000 for all Carmel Valley houses. They sold for between $2,175,000 and $3,835,000.

Which neighborhoods are in the Ashley Falls Elementary area?

Based on where homes have sold, the area includes Sonoma, Palisades, Huntington Heights, Estates, Palma Del Mar, Private Collection, Watercolors, Amador, and more. Sonoma has had the most sales. Boundaries can split a neighborhood, so check the exact address.

What middle and high school go with Ashley Falls Elementary?

High school is Torrey Pines High (San Dieguito Union HSD); Canyon Crest Academy and San Dieguito Academy are available by lottery. Middle school depends on the address, so confirm it with San Dieguito Union HSD.

How do I confirm a home is in the Ashley Falls Elementary boundary?

Use the Del Mar Union School District school locator with the exact address before you make an offer. Boundaries change, and this map is for research only.

How fast do homes sell in the Ashley Falls Elementary area?

Over the last 12 months, the median home sold in 10 days on market. Homes in sought-after school areas often go under contract in the first week.
