Pacific Sky Elementary Area Homes: Common Questions

How much does a house in the Pacific Sky Elementary area cost?

In the last 12 months, 16 single-family homes in the Pacific Sky Elementary attendance area sold at a median of $2,177,500 (about $805 per sq ft), versus $2,625,000 for all Carmel Valley houses. Over the last 5 years, 90 sold for between $1,270,000 and $4,150,000.

Which neighborhoods are in the Pacific Sky Elementary area?

Based on where homes have sold, the area includes Elms, Cassavia, Verana, Casabella, Canterra, Costa Del Sol, Sterling, Ivy, and more. Elms has had the most sales. Boundaries can split a neighborhood, so check the exact address.

What middle and high school go with Pacific Sky Elementary?

High school is Torrey Pines High (San Dieguito Union HSD); Canyon Crest Academy and San Dieguito Academy are available by lottery. Middle school depends on the address, so confirm it with San Dieguito Union HSD.

How do I confirm a home is in the Pacific Sky Elementary boundary?

Use the Del Mar Union School District school locator with the exact address before you make an offer. Boundaries change, and this map is for research only.

How fast do homes sell in the Pacific Sky Elementary area?

Over the last 12 months, the median home sold in 16 days on market. Homes in sought-after school areas often go under contract in the first week.
